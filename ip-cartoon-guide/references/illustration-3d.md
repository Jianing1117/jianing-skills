# 3D Real-object Scenes

## Formula

```text
confirmed 3D personal IP + one real object group + one physical action + 2-4 labels + white studio space
```

## Standard scene

- Default to 16:9 on a seamless `#FFFFFF` studio surface.
- Give the character and objects one light direction, perspective, scale, and subtle contact shadows.
- Use one main object group, at most two small props, one physical conflict, and 2-4 short Chinese labels.
- Keep the footprint medium-light, roughly 55%-70% of the width, with air between objects.
- The character must touch, pull, block, sort, carry, inspect, or otherwise drive the conflict; never paste it beside the scene.
- Avoid UI, logos, room backgrounds, cyber styling, grey gradients, product close-ups, collage edges, PPT layouts, and identity or material drift.

## Content design

Compress the idea into: “who is pushed by what, through which physical action, toward what result?” Use familiar objects that support pulling, overflow, review, relabeling, filtering, or pressure.

For a shot list, include only theme, reader situation, physical conflict, main object, character action, labels, three-second reading, and identity risk.

## Prompt requirements

Every image prompt must include:

- Confirmed 3D reference, identity anchors, material, proportions, light, and palette.
- One main object group, exact physical contact and force direction, and 2-4 exact labels.
- White studio integration, unified light/perspective/scale, whitespace, and explicit bans on flat-sticker placement and identity drift.

Use the complete standard template in `prompt-3d.md` when generating, especially with weaker models.

## Optional long scroll

Use only for an explicit growth path, personal story, project review, or product evolution request: `2.6:1`-`3:1`, near-white background, one organic route, 5-8 unevenly spaced real-object milestones, and the same 3D IP acting at every node. Use no numbering, card grid, regular wave, or invented facts.

Use the complete long-scroll template in `prompt-3d.md`.

## QA

Check only: same person and 3D material, physical integration, character-led action, one three-second conflict, restrained object budget, clean background, and short legible labels. Fix identity, integration, scale, action, or text separately.
